Abstract

Modern Large Language Models (LLMs) rely on sub-word tokenization algorithms—such as Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) and Unigram Language Modeling—alongside safety alignment trained predominantly on high-resource prose. This exploratory research note introduces Self-Intertwined Deconstructed Philoprompts (Philoprompts-v1.0), an experimental multilingual and Unicode test concept authored by Mehrdad Poorhassan (Dr. Metal). Each illustrative entry combines three conceptual layers: deconstructed Persian micro-poetics and syntactic perturbation, exploratory classical and Non-BMP ancient script renderings (Asokan Brahmi, Sumero-Akkadian Cuneiform, Old Persian, Avestan, Classical Chinese, Ancient Greek, Classical Latin, Classical Ge'ez, and Zargari cipher; draft renderings not independently reviewed by specialist philologists), and defensive LLM security analysis examining interactions with BPE UTF-8 4-byte fallback pathways and safety classifiers. Independent third-party validation is not claimed.
